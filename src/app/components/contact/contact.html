Cancellation

Order Cancellation

  • Only PENDING orders can be cancelled. Dispatched orders cannot be cancelled.
  • Fill the form with the exact details used while placing the order.
  • Cancellation is processed instantly once details are verified.

Refunds

Refund Policy

  • Refunds are credited to your original payment method within 5–7 working days.
  • Refunds are transferred via UPI or bank transfer.
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the issue was on our end.
  • Refund status updates will be sent to your registered email.

Quick Answers

Where do I find my Tracking Key?

Your Tracking Key is in your order confirmation email or SMS. It looks like: EUDTYNR6NLE6A — all capital letters and numbers.

My order is already dispatched — can I cancel?

Unfortunately, once your order is dispatched we cannot cancel it. Please contact us on WhatsApp or email for any assistance.

When will I get my refund?

Refund is processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method. For COD orders, we'll transfer via UPI or bank transfer.

Getting an error on the form?

Make sure your Tracking Key, Name, and Email exactly match what you entered while placing the order. Contact us on WhatsApp if the issue persists.
